# Phase 1: Newman Reconstructions (Dossiers 1-4)

**Status:** Completed
**Source:** John Henry Newman, *An Essay on the Development of Christian Doctrine*, Chapter V (Genuine Developments Contrasted with Corruptions)
**Witness Version:** `newman-ch5.html` / `newman-ch5.txt` (via NewmanReader.org)
**Schema:** `Claim → Historical Witness → Verified Quotation → Inference → Conclusion → Strongest Objection`

---

## Dossier 1: Preservation of Type (The Holy Trinity)

*   **Claim:** A genuine doctrinal development preserves the "type" or essential form of the original doctrine, even if it introduces vast internal distinctions that appear at first glance to obscure the original simplicity.
*   **Historical Witness:** Petavius (Denis Pétau), answering heretics who claimed the Trinity destroys Divine Unity.
*   **Verified Quotation:** "I will affirm, what perhaps will surprise the reader, that that distinction of Persons which, in regard to *proprietates* is in reality most great, is so far from disparaging the Unity and Simplicity of God that this very real distinction especially avails for the doctrine that God is One and most Simple." (Section 1.5)
*   **Context:** Newman is illustrating that physical organisms undergo extreme internal changes that preserve their overarching "type". He applies this to theology, noting that the complex articulation of the Trinity grew out of the simple doctrine of Divine Unity.
*   **Inference:** If a later theological formulation (Trinity) provides the precise structural mechanics necessary to protect an earlier, simpler axiom (Unity) from dissolution, it is a faithful development, not a corruption.
*   **Conclusion:** The complex Catholic doctrine of the Holy Trinity is a genuine development of the primitive belief in the Divine Unity.
*   **Strongest Objection:** The mathematical and logical distinction of three Persons fundamentally contradicts the absolute numerical simplicity of the One God, making the development destructive to the original premise.
*   **Unresolved Option:** Whether the psychological perception of "Unity" held by a 1st-century Christian is actually the same "type" as the highly technical metaphysical "Unity" defended by Petavius.

## Dossier 2: Continuity of Principles (Arianism vs. Eunomianism)

*   **Claim:** Doctrines can completely invert or contradict each other over time, yet remain faithful "developments" of the original movement, provided they share the same underlying *principle*.
*   **Historical Witness:** Arius (founder of Arianism) and Eunomius (founder of Eunomianism).
*   **Verified Quotation:** "Arius asserted that the Second Person of the Blessed Trinity was not able to comprehend the First, whereas Eunomius's characteristic tenet was in an opposite direction, viz., that not only the Son, but that all men could comprehend God; yet no one can doubt that Eunomianism was a true development, not a corruption of Arianism." (Section 1.5)
*   **Context:** Newman is distinguishing between *doctrines* (which are objective professions/facts) and *principles* (which are the underlying ethical or philosophical operating laws). 
*   **Inference:** Both Arius and Eunomius operated on the identical underlying principle: *there is no mystery in theology* (i.e., the divine nature must be entirely subject to human rationalist comprehension). Because the principle remained unbroken, the theological shift was a true development of the heretical school, despite the doctrines directly colliding.
*   **Conclusion:** Continuity of principle is a stronger test for the identity of a belief system than mere continuity of specific doctrines.
*   **Strongest Objection:** A system cannot be said to "faithfully develop" if its endpoint explicitly denies the foundational dogmatic claim made by its founder.
*   **Unresolved Option:** Whether "Continuity of Principles" can be rigorously measured by an AI agent, given that principles are often unstated philosophical priors (like "there is no mystery"), unlike explicit textual doctrines.

## Dossier 3: Logical Sequence (The Fate of Lutheranism)

*   **Claim:** An idea naturally develops along a logical sequence without the conscious planning of its adherents. If a system inherently contains contradictory principles, its eventual unraveling into infidelity is a *faithful development* of its flawed origins.
*   **Historical Witness:** Martin Luther, Wolf, Semler, Ernesti, and David Strauss.
*   **Verified Quotation:** "...the equable and orderly march and natural succession of views, by which the creed of Luther has been changed into the infidel or heretical philosophy of his present representatives, is a proof that that change is no perversion or corruption, but a faithful development of the original idea." (Section 4.4)
*   **Context:** Newman traces the history of Lutheranism. Luther started on a double basis: a dogmatic sacramental principle and the principle of private judgment. After his death, private judgment gained supremacy, leading logically to rationalism, which then dismantled scriptural authority entirely.
*   **Inference:** Because the final outcome (19th-century German rationalism/infidelity) was reached by a strict, step-by-step logical application of Luther's core premise (private judgment), the destruction of his church was not an accident or corruption, but the "fruit" of his seed.
*   **Conclusion:** The presence of a rigorous "Logical Sequence" over centuries proves that an endpoint is the true descendant of an origin, even if the founder would have abhorred the endpoint.
*   **Strongest Objection:** Luther himself vehemently condemned rationalism and held strictly to the physical reality of the sacraments; attributing Strauss's infidelity to Luther ignores Luther's actual historical boundaries.
*   **Unresolved Option:** If logical sequence alone dictates development, then any system containing a minor internal contradiction is pre-destined to total dissolution, negating the role of historical intervention or correction.

## Dossier 4: Conservative Action on its Past (The Catholic Creed)

*   **Claim:** A true development is conservative; it adds to, completes, and assimilates the truths that preceded it, rather than simply destroying them.
*   **Historical Witness:** St. Leo, Vincent of Lerins, and the structure of the Catholic Creed itself.
*   **Verified Quotation:** "True religion is the summit and perfection of false religions; it combines in one whatever there is of good and true separately remaining in each... true conversion is ever of a positive, not a negative character." (Section 6.2)
*   **Context:** Newman addresses how the Church handles paganism or heresy. Instead of pure negation, it extracts the fragmented truths hidden within false systems and integrates them into a unified whole.
*   **Inference:** A corruption reverses or destroys the acquisitions gained in previous history. A genuine development operates like a living organism assimilating food: it absorbs external truths and synthesizes them into its own living tissue without losing its identity.
*   **Conclusion:** The Seventh Note of a genuine development is its ability to conserve its past while expanding, proving that "grace does not destroy nature, but perfects it."
*   **Strongest Objection:** The historical Church *did* aggressively destroy pagan institutions and burn heretical texts, demonstrating highly destructive, non-conservative action toward rival systems.
*   **Unresolved Option:** Distinguishing between theological assimilation (which is conservative) and political/institutional action (which is often destructive), and determining which is the true marker of "doctrinal development."
